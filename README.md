# Kerala Journey Connect

Rosses Ananda — Website Development Master Prompt

1. Project Overview

Build a premium, warm, trustworthy and highly personal website for Rosses Ananda, a Kerala-based Ayurveda and wellness travel service.

Rosses Ananda helps primarily Czech and European clients experience authentic Ayurveda in Kerala, India. The business personally coordinates Ayurvedic programmes, trusted Ayurvedic doctors and centres, accommodation, airport transfers, local experiences and ongoing support throughout the client's stay.

The website must communicate that Rosses Ananda is not simply an Ayurvedic treatment booking service.

The central value proposition is:

Having someone you personally trust in Kerala who takes care of the details of your Ayurvedic journey.

The website should make a prospective client feel safe, understood and confident about travelling to Kerala.

2. Primary Website Goals

The website should:

Establish trust immediately.

Explain what Rosses Ananda does clearly.

Introduce the person behind Rosses Ananda.

Explain the Ayurvedic programmes and experiences.

Present current Ayurvedic doctors and professional partner centres.

Explain how the entire journey is personally coordinated.

Showcase Kerala as part of the experience.

Generate enquiries from potential clients.

Make contacting Rosses Ananda through WhatsApp/email extremely easy.

Work exceptionally well on mobile devices.

Feel premium without looking corporate or impersonal.

The primary conversion should be:

Enquire / Plan Your Ayurveda Journey

The website should not feel like an e-commerce store unless a future requirement specifically introduces online booking or payment.

3. Target Audience

Primary audience:

People from the Czech Republic

European travellers

People interested in authentic Ayurveda in Kerala

Wellness travellers

People seeking detox and Panchakarma

People interested in rejuvenation

People looking for longer Ayurveda stays

People who want to combine Ayurveda with relaxation and exploring Kerala

Potential clients may have very different needs.

Some may want a specific Ayurvedic treatment.

Others may want:

Ayurveda + relaxation + accommodation + Kerala experiences.

Therefore, avoid presenting the service as a single rigid package.

The experience should appear personalised according to each client's needs.

26. Content Management

Do not hard-code information unnecessarily.

Doctor profiles, programmes, accommodation and testimonials should preferably come from structured data objects.

For example:

doctors = [
  {
    name,
    image,
    qualifications,
    experience,
    specialisation,
    description
  }
]


This should make it easy to add or remove doctors later without rebuilding the entire UI.

36. AI Coding Instructions

If you are an AI coding agent implementing this website:

Do not invent business facts.

Do not invent doctors, credentials, testimonials, prices or locations.

Use placeholders for missing information.

Build reusable components.

Keep the code clean and maintainable.

Do not over-engineer the project.

Prioritise responsive design.

Prioritise accessibility.

Prioritise performance.

Do not add unnecessary libraries.

Do not create a database unless required.

Keep content easy to replace.

Use semantic HTML.

Ensure all navigation links work.

Ensure the enquiry CTA is prominent.

Ensure WhatsApp/contact functionality can easily be configured.

Never use old photographs from the previous doctor/partner.

Clearly distinguish confirmed content from placeholders.

Do not make medical claims that have not been provided or verified.

Preserve the feeling of personal human connection throughout the website.

37. Final Design Objective

The finished website should make a European visitor feel:

"I understand what Rosses Ananda offers."

Then:

"This feels authentic and personal."

Then:

"I can trust this person to help me in Kerala."

And finally:

"I would like to contact them and discuss my Ayurveda journey."

The website should therefore sell confidence, personal care, authenticity and connection before it sells individual treatments.

Build the experience around the person, the Ayurvedic professionals, Kerala and the client's journey — not around generic wellness marketing.
